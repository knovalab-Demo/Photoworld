About Studio Photoworld

We Frame Stories With Emotion, Style, and Precision.

Studio Photoworld by Vijay Amate is a photography studio based in Akola, specializing in weddings, portraits, and cinematic shoots. With years of experience and a passion for storytelling, we focus on creating photographs that feel elegant, honest, and deeply memorable.
